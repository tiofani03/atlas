Design Guidelines & Technical Compliance

All 4 logo concepts strictly satisfy every constraint of the design specification:

No Clichés & Flat Vectors

Zero gradients, zero drop shadows, no text, no robot heads, brain icons, clouds, or generic AI symbols. Pure geometric lines and vector shapes.

Network Graph + Compass

Circular construction merging cardinal navigation (North/East/South/West) with graph vertices (nodes & structural edges) symbolizing connected engineering context.

Multi-Scale Legibility

Engineered for high silhouette clarity at 16x16 menu/favicon sizes as well as full 512x512 app icons, dark modes, and 100% monochrome prints.
